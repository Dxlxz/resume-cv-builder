# Gold Standard Guide: Resume vs CV Architecture & Section Blueprint

> **Reference Standard:** Malaysian Corporate, MNCs (Oil & Gas / Energy / Tech), TalentCorp Malaysia, JobStreet by SEEK, Harvard Career Services, Google XYZ Formula.

---

## 1. Executive Summary: Resume vs Curriculum Vitae (CV)

| Dimension | Resume (Curated & Targeted) | Curriculum Vitae (CV - Comprehensive) |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Purpose** | High-impact marketing document targeted to a specific job opening. | Complete, exhaustive historical record of academic and professional career. |
| **Strict Page Limit** | **Maximum 1–2 Pages (Hard Limit)** for early/mid-career; never 3. | **Multi-Page (3–6+ Pages)** depending on project/research volume. |
| **Skill Strategy** | **Curated (15–20 high-signal tools/skills)** grouped logically. | **Complete Inventory (40–60+ tools, frameworks, protocols)**. |
| **Project Strategy** | Top 2–3 most relevant projects with quantified impact. | Full project registry, research papers, capstone, client deliveries. |
| **Best Used For** | Job fairs (SICC), corporate portals (Workday, JobStreet), MNC interviews. | Academic applications, government tenders, GLC talent pools, PhD/MSc admissions. |

## 2. Section-by-Section Gold Standard Blueprint

```
                     RESUME HIERARCHY BLUEPRINT (2 PAGES)
  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ 1. HEADER & CONTACT    (Name, Targeted Headline, Location, Phone, Links)│
  ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 2. PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY (4-line Elevator Pitch + Immediate Availability)│
  ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 3. WORK EXPERIENCE      (Reverse Chronological, Google XYZ / CAR format)│
  ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 4. EDUCATION            (Degree, Major, CGPA, Dean's List, Key Modules) │
  ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 5. CERTIFICATIONS       (Recognised Industry Credentials, Issuers, Year)│
  ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 6. SKILLS ARCHITECTURE  (Categorised: Domain, Languages/DBs, Cloud/Ops) │
  ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 7. FEATURED PROJECTS    (2-3 high-impact technical deliveries + GitHub) │
  ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 8. LEADERSHIP & SERVICE (Elected Council, National Youth Delegations)   │
  ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 9. REFERENCES           (Verified Supervisor / GM, Contact Details)     │
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Section 1: Header & Contact Information

* **Gold Standard Rules:**
  * **Targeted Headline:** Must reflect the target role and core stack (e.g. *Data Science Engagement Lead \| Python ML · SHAP · Power BI · Business Partnering*), not a generic title.
  * **Phone Format:** International format with country code (e.g. `+60 12-345 6789`).
  * **Location Clarity:** City, State, Country (e.g. *Kota Kinabalu, Sabah, Malaysia*). If open to relocation, state: *Open to Relocation (KL / Labuan / Miri / Nationwide)*.
  * **Direct Links:** Clean URLs for LinkedIn (`linkedin.com/in/...`) and GitHub (`github.com/...`).
  * **ATS Warning:** Never place contact details inside Microsoft Word headers/footers or image boxes—ATS parsers frequently ignore header/footer streams.

---

### Section 2: Executive / Professional Summary

* **Structure (The 4-Sentence Rule):**
  1. **Sentence 1 (Identity & Academic Standing):** Degree, institution, CGPA / Dean's List, and total years of hands-on experience.
  2. **Sentence 2 (Core Engineering / Business Impact):** Proven capability in primary technical domain (e.g. building data pipelines, ML explainability, government dashboards).
  3. **Sentence 3 (Leadership & Soft Skills):** Student council governance, stakeholder communication, or cross-functional delivery.
  4. **Sentence 4 (Value Proposition & Logistics):** Target role, immediate availability, and relocation readiness.
* **Tone:** Confident, proactive, third-person executive tone (zero first-person pronouns: no "I", "me", "my").

---

### Section 3: Work Experience (The Google XYZ & Harvard CAR Framework)

* **Formula:**
  $$\text{Accomplished } [X] \text{ as measured by } [Y] \text{ by doing } [Z]$$
  *(Context $\rightarrow$ Action $\rightarrow$ Result)*

* **Golden Bullet Examples:**
  * ❌ *Weak / Passive:* "Responsible for building Power BI dashboards for government clients."
  * ✅ *High-Impact (Google XYZ):* "Delivered three government Power BI reporting dashboards in a three-person technical team to support municipal and state executive decisions."
  * ❌ *Weak / Passive:* "Helped with rates database testing."
  * ✅ *High-Impact (Google XYZ):* "Executed system integration testing (SIT) and user acceptance testing (UAT) for municipal rates analytics (GeoRAS), delivering verified reporting views to DBKK."
* **Seniority Rule:** 3 bullet points per job for junior/mid roles. Lead with strong past-tense action verbs for past roles (*Architected*, *Engineered*, *Delivered*, *Optimised*) and present-tense for current positions (*Engineers*, *Maintains*).

---

### Section 4: Education & Academic Pedigree

* **Essential Elements:**
  * Institution Name & Location (e.g. *Universiti Malaysia Sabah, Kota Kinabalu, Sabah*).
  * Degree Title & Major (e.g. *BSc (Hons) Computer Science (Data Science)*).
  * Completion Dates (`Sep 2021 – Nov 2025`).
  * **Honors / Merit:** State CGPA if $\ge 3.00$ (e.g. *CGPA 3.23*), *Dean's List (Semesters 2 & 8)*.
  * **Final Year Project (FYP):** Title and technologies (e.g. *MathMentor: web app, linear regression ML, gamification*).
  * **Relevant Coursework:** High-value modules (*Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics, Statistical Computing, Distributed Systems*).

---

### Section 5: Certifications & Licences

* **Local Relevance for Malaysia:**
  * Industry Platform Certifications (e.g. *n8n Automation Official*, *ArcGIS Pro Essential Esri*).
  * Language & Workplace Certifications (e.g. *Essential Business English for Workplace Skills — ELS / JobStreet*).
  * Driving Licence (Class D / Own Transport) for site, field, or operations engineering roles.

---

### Section 6: Skills Architecture (Un-cluttering & Categorisation)

* **Why Single-Line Dumps Fail:**
  Listing 50+ comma-separated tools creates visual clutter ("semak") and reduces recruiter retention.
* **The 4-Category Blueprint:**
  1. **Specialisms & Domain:** High-level capabilities (*Geospatial Intelligence, Data Engineering, System Integration Testing (SIT/UAT), Business Intelligence*).
  2. **Programming & Databases:** Core code and storage engines (*Python, PostgreSQL, T-SQL, TypeScript, dbt*).
  3. **Platforms & DevOps:** Infrastructure and visualization tools (*Power BI, Apache Superset, Docker, Azure Cloud, n8n, Git / CI/CD*).
  4. **Languages:** Professional fluency (*English (Full Professional), Malay (Native / Professional)*).

---

### Section 7: Featured Projects

* **Format:**
  * Project Title + Scope Tag (*EarthData Hub [Data Governance Platform]*).
  * Technologies Used (*TypeScript, Hono, React, PostgreSQL, Docker*).
  * Direct GitHub URL (e.g. `github.com/username/project-name`).
  * 1–2 bullet points highlighting architecture, explainability (e.g. SHAP), or business utility.

---

### Section 8: Leadership & Co-Curricular (The "Well-Rounded" Pillar)

* **Why Top Employers (Shell, PETRONAS, GLCs) Care:**
  Technical competence gets you shortlisted; demonstrated leadership and resilience gets you hired.
* **Highlights:**
  * Elected Student Representative Council (MPP) leadership.
  * National Parliamentary youth delegations (*Parlimen Mahasiswa Kebangsaan — PMK 2023*).

---

### Section 9: References

* **Standard Practice in Malaysia:**
  * 1 verified professional referee (Direct Manager / General Manager) with title, company, phone, and professional email.
  * Alternatively: *"Available upon request"* for 1-page compact resumes.
